🌱 About

SIH-RuralEdge-AI is an AI-powered platform designed to support rural communities through intelligent guidance, financial analysis, government scheme discovery and accessible digital services.

It brings multiple capabilities together into a single decision-support platform, helping users access relevant information and make better-informed decisions.

✨ Key Capabilities

Capability| Description
🤖 AI Advisory| Personalized AI-powered guidance
💰 Financial Analysis| Financial feasibility and decision support
🏛️ Scheme Discovery| Government scheme identification and matching
📍 Geo Intelligence| Location-based recommendations
🗣️ Voice Advisory| Voice-based interaction
🌐 Multi-Language| Accessible multilingual experience
📊 Feasibility Dashboard| Visual financial and decision insights
📄 PDF Services| Generate useful reports and documents

---

🎯 Problem

Rural communities often face challenges when accessing information, financial guidance and government support.

Challenge| Description
🏛️ Government Schemes| Difficulty discovering relevant schemes and benefits
💰 Financial Decisions| Limited access to personalized financial guidance
🌐 Digital Accessibility| Digital services can be difficult to access or navigate
🗣️ Language Barriers| Language and literacy can limit digital accessibility
📍 Local Relevance| Generic information may not reflect local needs

---

💡 Solution

RuralEdge-AI combines AI, financial analysis, scheme matching, location intelligence and accessible interaction into one platform.

Instead of users searching across multiple sources, the platform aims to provide relevant insights and actionable guidance in one place.

🔄 Decision Support Workflow

flowchart LR
    A[👤 User Requirements] --> B[🤖 AI Analysis]
    B --> C[💰 Financial Analysis]
    C --> D[🏛️ Scheme Matching]
    D --> E[📍 Geo Intelligence]
    E --> F[🧠 AI Advisory]
    F --> G[📊 Feasibility Dashboard]
    G --> H[✅ Actionable Decision]
