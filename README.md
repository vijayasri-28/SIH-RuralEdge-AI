## 🌱 About

**SIH-RuralEdge-AI** is an AI-powered platform designed to support rural communities with intelligent guidance, financial analysis, government scheme discovery and accessible digital services.

The platform brings multiple services together into a single decision-support system.

### What it provides

| 🌟 Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| 🤖 AI Advisory | Personalized AI-powered guidance |
| 💰 Financial Analysis | Financial feasibility and decision support |
| 🏛️ Scheme Discovery | Government scheme identification and matching |
| 📍 Geo Intelligence | Location-based recommendations |
| 🗣️ Voice Advisory | Voice-based interaction |
| 🌐 Multi-Language | Accessible multilingual experience |
| 📊 Feasibility Dashboard | Visual financial and decision insights |
| 📄 PDF Services | Generate and manage useful reports |

---

## 🎯 Problem

Rural communities often face difficulties accessing information, financial guidance and government support.

| 🏛️ Government Schemes | 💰 Financial Decisions |
|---|---|
| Difficulty discovering relevant government schemes and benefits. | Limited access to personalized financial guidance and feasibility analysis. |

| 🌐 Digital Accessibility | 🗣️ Language & Voice |
|---|---|
| Limited digital access and awareness in rural areas. | Language and literacy barriers can make digital services difficult to use. |

---

## 💡 Solution

RuralEdge-AI combines AI, financial analysis, scheme matching, location intelligence and accessible interaction into one platform.
